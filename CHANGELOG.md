## 1.13.0

  use choice-and-scope dialogs and describe conversation participants as Who answers.

  automatically after new instructions are reviewed. Missing bookkeeping never
  counts as completion; cancellation and changed scope take precedence.

  reviews. Preserve blocking diagnostics for incomplete or unverified reviews.

  With Modes installed, mirror its activity while retaining partnership gates.

  by default for new Claude chats, preserving resume/compaction and saved history.
- Use macOS-contained research scripts with isolated HOME/caches, credential-read restrictions and networking;

- Add Modes to the HTTPS marketplace. Canonical state schema remains 18.

  partners. Only an explicit stop cancels. Mid-turn messages reach Codex as their own cycle.

Known limit: networked research scripts cannot be technically prevented from remote
submissions; this is a documented rule and no credentials reach them.
